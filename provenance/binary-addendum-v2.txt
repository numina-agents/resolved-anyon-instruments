Proposition (resource comparison).
Let G be a finite group with a two-dimensional irreducible quaternionic
unitary representation V. In Z(Vec_G), allow the fully resolved protocol's
vacuum-pair creation and routing, and ADD nondestructive binary vacuum versus
nonvacuum Luders measurement with coherent failed sector. The SUBMODEL using only vacuum X pairs, routing and binary PAIR tests
is BQP-universal, for the pure-electric simple X=(1,V). In particular
G=Q8 gives a nilpotency-class-two example. No polarized calibrated ancillary
states or free postselection are needed.

Proof.
The pure-electric full symmetric subcategory is Rep(G), with its usual tensor
representation inner products. Quaternionic irreducibility means the unique
invariant in V tensor V is an antisymmetric nondegenerate bilinear pairing.
Since dimV=2, its normalized invariant vector is the ordinary two-qubit singlet
under a unitary identification V=C^2. Equivalently, Lambda^2V is the trivial
representation and Sym^2V has no invariant. Therefore the ACTUAL vacuum
charge projector on X tensor X is exactly P_s, and the extra binary instrument
is exactly {P_s,I-P_s}, with its full three-dimensional triplet sector coherent.

Create independent X,X vacuum pairs. Keep one member of each pair active and
never interact with its partner. The reduced active input is (I/2) tensor N.
No physical trace/discard resource is necessary: the inactive partners simply
purify the mixed qubits and remain part of the total-vacuum state. The same
purification argument applies whenever a simulated protocol discards a qubit:
never interact with it again and ignore it in classical output processing.
The total number of physical anyons is polynomial in the simulated resources.

On any chosen pair of active X anyons, perform the binary vacuum measurement.
Its reduced action is exactly the singlet/triplet measurement of Rudolph and
Virmani, arXiv2107.03239v3 (2023), whose theorem is STP=BQP with initially
maximally mixed qubits and adaptive pairwise nondestructive s/t measurements.
Their complete bounded-error polynomial-resource computation is therefore
reproduced, with every actual outcome sampled; none is freely postselected.
Routing arbitrary pairs has polynomial overhead using symmetric pure-electric
braids. Every operator is G-equivariant, so the full purified state stays in
the physical TOTAL-VACUUM sector. This pair-only submodel is itself contained in BQP: its pair operations have
constant-dimensional circuit realizations. No upper-complexity claim for every
extra arbitrary-disk oracle is required for the universality reduction.
