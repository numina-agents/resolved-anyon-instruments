## Verdict

The entire mathematical account in the frozen combined source is CORRECT,
including its classical sampling theorem, class-two corollary and precisely
stated binary pair-test BQP-completeness reduction. I found no
material gap requiring a change to its statement or proof. The exact prior
comparison supports the stated novelty boundary, and I judge the whole endpoint
substantial enough for a research paper. It gives a uniform compilation of all
specified intrinsic instruments across all abelian twisted finite gauge
theories, with the class-two untwisted corollary. The standard Heisenberg,
normalizer-simulation and Morita results are ingredients, not new claims.

For the positive theorem, the resources are fully resolved simple-charge Lüders
measurements and caps on an already known vacuum channel. For Proposition 6,
the additional coherent binary PAIR measurement is included, and the claim is
BQP-completeness of that specified pair-only submodel. This also proves that a
larger model containing it is universal; it does not prove an upper bound for
arbitrary extra disk oracles. Neither theorem grants arbitrary ancillary
states, phase changes, handles or free rare-transcript postselection. There is
no unconditional computational separation, and the positive algorithm is not
claimed uniform in the category size.

The STP=BQP theorem is wholly PRIOR Rudolph--Virmani. The quaternionic and
purification reduction is a short corollary within the same account, not a
separate new universality programme. Its role is to make the main resource
boundary computationally sharp in the same class-two phase Q8.

The packet is a review candidate, not an authorized publication. The director
decides the publication handoff. The publication bibliography should incorporate
the additional exact Van den Nest 2011 and Magna credits discussed below;
these are bibliographic recommendations, not corrections to the mathematics.

## Correctness of the complete mechanism

For a finite abelian group A and unitary two-cocycle beta, its commutator B is
alternating. If R is its radical, the quotient A/R has a perfect alternating
pairing. Choosing an element of maximum order and a partner with primitive
pairing splits a hyperbolic cyclic pair; induction proves the asserted
K plus K-dual decomposition, including even and mixed exponents. This is a
decomposition of the quotient and does NOT require A to split over R.

Pulling the standard Weyl multiplier back to A gives a cocycle c with the same
commutator. The symmetric cocycle beta/c is a coboundary: its abelian central
extension splits because U(1) is injective. Thus q times a Weyl action gives
every irreducible projective representation after multiplying by a character
extending a character of R. The normalized central units q(r)^(-1)e_r form
C[R]; each central-character block is a matrix algebra of dimension |A/R|.
Consequently all irreducibles have dimension sqrt(|A/R|), and each charge image
projectively surjects onto the full Weyl group. The argument does not assume
free modules over a prime-power ring or invertibility of 2.

The published DPR formulas used in the source give the displayed beta_a and
gamma_x(a,b), scalar associators on fixed flux words, and braiding equal to
swap followed by a single charge operator. For a tensor word, the actual charge
action, with all coproduct scalars, has multiplier beta_(sum flux). This is
essential: no additivity of the arbitrary coboundaries q_a is assumed.

On the radical of that total multiplier, U(r)=q_a(r)^(-1)rho(r) is an ordinary
commuting representation by scalar Paulis. Its character average is precisely
the full central isotypic projector. A tuple of eigenvalues on generators
determines exactly one central character and hence exactly one simple charge
at the deterministic flux. Sequential generator measurements therefore have
the same Kraus projector as the desired simple-charge outcome. They reveal
no vector or multiplicity label. Tensor-factor entanglement with the outside
register does not change this operator identity.

At total flux zero the global vacuum space is a joint Pauli eigenspace.
Subdisk radical operators commute with the global action. Scalar reassociation
and the swap/Pauli braid formulas make every specified routed disk a Clifford
conjugate of the same instrument in the persistent elementary registers.
The routing complexity is included in L. This is a simultaneous statement
about arbitrary sequences of overlapping disk measurements, not individual
Gaussian realizations in bases chosen anew after each measurement.

The dual charge gamma_x(a,-a)^(-1) times the conjugate of pi has exactly
multiplier beta_(-a); the coproduct relation proves this for arbitrary twists.
The Bell vector is invariant. An intertwiner with the already chosen dual
simple is unitary and normalizes the full projective Pauli group, since both
projective images are full Weyl groups. This proves it is Clifford without
assuming a Clifford gate synthesis theorem. It also works when a=-a and when
the simple is self-dual of symplectic type. Normalizing the physical cup leaves
only an irrelevant scalar phase. A known vacuum pair is a one-dimensional
Bell channel and factors from the remaining pure register. Its cap is the
inverse stabilizer isometry. For simulation one can either eliminate that
known factor by linear algebra or keep its registers inactive; the latter uses
at most O(L) registers and already gives the claimed polynomial bound.

Root-of-unity cocycle representatives exist. The finite symmetric-coboundary
and character-extension phase equations admit rational phase solutions, obtained
by integer linear algebra, so preprocessing and all subsequent labels can be
exact. Conjugation by the fixed dual intertwiners is computed through the
projective surjection rather than their matrix entries or a synthesis search.
The generalized stabilizer update and sampling proofs apply to this supplied
conjugation data. Variable register products have log dimension O(n), or O(L)
if inactive cap registers are retained. There are polynomially many operations
in the finite circuit description. Exact finite-uniform random choices have
constant-success rejection samplers; truncating their exponential tails gives
the stated bounded polynomial runtime at total-variation error epsilon.
No rare-transcript postselection is used.

For class-two G, H=Z(G) is an abelian normal subgroup and the trivial module
cochain satisfies Naidu's pointed-dual criteria. The action on H-dual is trivial
and the auxiliary extension cocycle is trivial. The dual group is therefore
H-dual times G/H, an abelian group; Naidu's associator specializes to the
character evaluation on the central extension cocycle stated in the source.
The equivalence is of fusion categories, not just fusion rings. Passing to
centres and using Reutter gives a unitary braided equivalence. It preserves
the canonical unitary ribbon structures, normalized vacuum maps and orthogonal
simple-isotypic projectors. Only fixed simple-label and local equivalence data
are needed to transfer the protocol; no exponentially large tensorator matrix
needs to be constructed during a simulation.

Finally the separate binary failure vector has support 2p^2-p and probability
1-p^(-2). Its total-vacuum assertion and support count are correct. For odd p
this support is not a power of p, excluding a Pauli stabilizer state in that
encoding. This proves exactly a failure of the positive state-class closure
under an additional instrument, as claimed, and proves no hardness statement.

## The binary pair-test reduction in the combined source

For a two-dimensional irreducible quaternionic representation V, Schur's lemma
gives a unique invariant in V tensor V and its Frobenius--Schur type makes it
antisymmetric. The entire one-dimensional exterior square is therefore the
trivial representation, and the normalized invariant is the usual singlet.
The actual orthogonal vacuum projector in the pure-electric Rep(G) subcategory
is exactly P_s, not merely an abstract rank-one projector in a separately
chosen fusion basis. Its complement is the full coherent triplet projector.
Changing the unitary basis of V does not change this antisymmetric line and
is not being supplied as an additional physical operation.

Independent physical singlet pairs give independent active marginal states
I/2 when one partner of each pair is permanently inactive. If M_h denotes a
product of active Kraus operators along an adaptive history, then tracing the
inactive registers in (M_h tensor I)|Psi><Psi|(M_h^* tensor I) gives exactly
M_h rho M_h^*. This proves equality of every outcome probability and active
conditional state, by induction even with classical feedforward. Later
simulated discards can also be implemented by permanent inactivity. No actual
partial trace, calibrated state or free successful branch is required.

All the pair projectors are G-equivariant. Thus every nonzero full pure branch
remains in Hom_G(1,V^(tensor 2m)), although its active marginal can be mixed.
Pure-electric braids are swaps, so arbitrary active pair access costs only
polynomial routing; inactive partners need not interact. The number of fresh
partners and abandoned systems is polynomial in the prior STP construction's
resources. This is an exact instrument reduction, so its bounded-error success
and polynomial preparation/accuracy overhead carry over. The model itself is
in BQP: singlets, swaps and a two-qubit singlet/triplet test have constant-size
quantum circuit implementations (for example the controlled-SWAP test). That
upper bound is only for the stated submodel.

For the displayed Q8 representation, rho(i)rho(j)=rho(k), each generator
squares to -I and the group average is exactly
(I tensor I-X tensor X-Y tensor Y-Z tensor Z)/4. The three nontrivial linear
characters together span the triplet and are individually the other three
Bell lines. Therefore the same class-two phase has the two different
instrument models claimed in the source. Forgetting a resolved Bell outcome
would dephase these lines and would not implement the binary Lüders failure
operator. This does not contradict the positive theorem.
