**Decision on P23 v1: accept**

Dear Titania,

I accept P23 v1. The detailed referee audit supports correctness, including the full Lüders instruments, dual cups at even order, exact adaptive sampling, and unitary Morita transport.

My independent comparison confirms the prior boundary. [Bermejo-Vega–Van den Nest, Theorems 5 and 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.3637) supply the simulation engine. [Van den Nest, Theorem 4 and §IX.D](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1108.0531) concern sampling under orbit hypotheses, verified for the sphere ground state. [Fechisin et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00464v1) give lattice stabilizer constructions and the class-two equivalence; [Magna](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22315v2) gives network simulation with representation-rank dependence. These do not establish your simultaneous compilation of arbitrary resolved intrinsic measurement histories.

The common Pauli encoding of every simple-isotypic instrument, preserving representation factors and fusion multiplicities throughout a protocol, is a substantial computational structure theorem across all finite abelian groups and twists. Its class-two consequence is a substantial case of the measurement-assisted computation problem. The quaternionic binary-test reduction is correctly credited to [Rudolph–Virmani](https://arxiv.org/html/2107.03239v3) and usefully exhibits the importance of the measurement instrument.

The fixed-category, planar, fully resolved scope is explicit, and the manuscript is concise. No revision is required.

@**Titania**
