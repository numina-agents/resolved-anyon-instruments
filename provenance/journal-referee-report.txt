Referee report: P23 v1, “Resolved and binary charge measurements in finite gauge theory”

Verdict: correct. I checked every proof in the submitted version, the structural formulas it uses, and the hypotheses of its cited results. I found no mathematical errors and no proof steps that remain unverified. This verdict concerns correctness only.

The distinction between the two instruments is essential and is consistently maintained: resolving all simple charges uses one isotypic projector per outcome, whereas the added binary instrument uses a single coherent projector onto the entire nonvacuum sum. The positive simulation result and the binary pair-test universality result therefore concern different permitted operations.

Proof audit (line numbers refer to the submitted paper.tex):

1. Lemma 3, simultaneous Weyl models (lines 101–137). I checked that the cocycle identity makes B an alternating bicharacter and that its radical quotient is nondegenerate. The hyperbolic splitting argument works also at even and mixed orders: an element of maximal order d has a partner with a primitive d-th-root pairing; both generate independent cyclic factors, and their nondegenerate subgroup splits off by orthogonal projection. Iteration gives K plus its character group. The pulled-back Weyl operators have the required commutator and are the identity on the radical. The quotient of their multiplier into beta is symmetric; the resulting abelian extension splits because U(1) is injective. The same argument extends every radical character. I checked the normalization q(r)^{-1}e_r and its multiplication directly. The twisted algebra is semisimple by the usual averaging argument. It is free over its central group algebra on representatives of A/R, so each central-character block has dimension |A/R| and scalar center. Consequently it is a full matrix algebra of degree sqrt(|A/R|). The displayed Weyl representation has full matrix image, realizes the prescribed central character, and exhausts the irreducibles up to equivalence. No splitting of R inside A is assumed or needed.

2. Structural formulas (lines 139–157). I compared the multiplication, coproduct, and R-matrix in the published ERW Section 3 with the abelian specializations used here. They give precisely beta_a, gamma_x(a,b), and flip composed with I tensor rho_Y(a). Bantay’s Appendix, equations (A.2)–(A.3), (A.11), and (A.13)–(A.18), supplies the stated inverse-cocycle convention. The associator is scalar on a fixed elementary-flux word. In particular, writing delta gamma_{a,b}(x,y)=gamma_x(a,b)gamma_y(a,b)/gamma_{x+y}(a,b), the multiplier identity is

   beta_{a+b}(x,y) = delta gamma_{a,b}(x,y) beta_a(x,y) beta_b(x,y).

This also implies B_{a+b}=B_a B_b and B_{-a}=B_a^{-1}. These are the identities needed later, including for the dual construction.

3. Lemma 4 and the persistent physical encoding (lines 160–207). The normalized central action U is an ordinary unitary representation of R_a by commuting scalar Paulis. Character orthogonality gives equation (3), and directly gives self-adjointness, idempotence, pairwise orthogonality, and completeness. Its restriction to each simple block is either the identity or zero; hence it retains both the representation factor and every multiplicity. A character is determined by its values on any generating set of R_a, so sequential measurement of those commuting operators has exactly this joint projector as its Kraus operator. It cannot supply finer information within an isotype. Scalar relabeling does not change that conclusion. At total flux zero, the vacuum space is the joint +1 eigenspace of the global charge action. A subdisk’s central operators commute with that action: their commutators are the product of the local B_{a_i}(r,x), namely B_a(r,x)=1. Reassociation, braids, their inverses, and the supplied routing words therefore preserve the same Pauli framework. There is no implicit fusion-basis refinement or change of ambient register after a measurement.

4. Lemma 5, cups and caps (lines 210–244). Substituting the multiplier identity above shows that gamma_x(a,-a)^{-1} conjugate(pi(x)) has multiplier beta_{-a}. The unitary identity (U tensor conjugate(U)) Bell=Bell then gives the vacuum cup. Irreducibility makes its invariant line unique. The inverse commutator forms allow the same abstract K for a and -a. A unitary intertwiner between the chosen dual model and the conjugate model conjugates their projectively surjective Pauli images onto one another, so it is Clifford. This remains true for self-dual objects and for 2-torsion; an antisymmetric intertwiner causes no obstruction. I checked the stated Bell stabilizers and their uniqueness. On a known vacuum pair the state factors across that pair and its complement. Undoing the cup Clifford and removing this known factor implements the normalized cap. The surviving stabilizers can be obtained by solving for combinations with trivial labels on the removed factors, so removal also admits an efficient stabilizer update.

5. Theorem 1, compilation and sampling (lines 247–279). ERW Lemma 4.3 supplies a root-of-unity cocycle representative; it may also be normalized. The finite coboundary congruences have integer coefficients and rational right-hand sides, and their known solvability gives rational phase solutions, for example by Smith normal form. Character extensions can likewise be chosen with finite-order phases. Thus all needed scalar arithmetic is exact fixed-category arithmetic. The number of cyclic register factors is O(n), and their orders are bounded by category-dependent constants. Creation, routing, measurement, and removal all have polynomial descriptions and updates. Induction starts from the empty vacuum and gives a single pure stabilizer state on every nonzero transcript.

I read BV’s Pauli measurement construction and update theorem, and its simulation proof. Although its circuit theorem is phrased for normalizer gates, the unitary update part uses only the efficiently supplied conjugation of stabilizer generators. The extension to the explicit Clifford data in this paper is valid; no unproved normalizer-gate decomposition assertion is being imported. Pauli measurement updates and their sampling apply to arbitrary stabilizer inputs. The number of measured generators per disk is bounded by the fixed A. Finally, uniform sampling from a finite interval by rejection from random bits has acceptance probability at least 1/2. Truncating sufficiently many attempts and taking a union bound over the polynomially many draws gives the claimed bounded-time total-variation approximation. The argument never conditions the simulation on obtaining a rare transcript.

6. Corollary 2 (lines 281–297). I checked Naidu’s pointed-dual hypotheses with H=Z(G), omega=1, and the trivial cochain. The dual is pointed, the quotient action on the character group is trivial, and the extra extension cocycle for its simple-object group is trivial. Naidu’s formula (24) reduces to eta((chi,x),(psi,y),(xi,z))=chi(kappa(y,z)), with simple-object group Z-hat times G/Z. This is abelian for the stated class-two hypothesis. Naidu’s Remark 5.5 supplies the consequence for centers; Fechisin Theorem D.1 independently records the forward equivalence used here. Reutter Theorem 5 applies because both centers are unitary braided fusion categories. Its unitary equivalence preserves the normalized invariant lines, braids, and orthogonal isotypic projectors, so it preserves the full instruments and probabilities. Fixed-category relabeling does not create a complexity overhead depending on the protocol size. The application to UT_3(F_p) has the stated hypotheses.

7. Explicit controls and binary failure (lines 299–330). For C4^3, I recomputed the radical at 2e_1 as C4 times C2 times C2, with simple-block dimension 2. In the indicated tensor representation, e_1 acts trivially and the other two radical generators act as the two commuting order-two Paulis stated. Their four joint projectors have trace/rank 4, giving multiplicity 2. The F2^3 multiplier and the antisymmetric XZ dual intertwiner satisfy the asserted equations. For the middle-pair binary test I independently contracted the Bell indices, obtaining exactly the displayed 1/p^2 sum. Its support intersects the original p^2-term support in p entries, with nonzero difference coefficient (p-1)/p^2 there. Hence the failed support has size 2p^2-p and squared norm 1-p^{-2}. This size is not a power of p, whereas a prime-qudit stabilizer’s support is an affine subspace. The vacuum projector commutes with the global charge action, so both branches remain in total vacuum. This proves precisely the claimed failure of Pauli closure, without implying hardness by itself.

8. Proposition 6 and Q8 (lines 340–403). In the pure-electric Rep(G) subcategory the braiding is the ordinary swap. Schur’s lemma and quaternionicity give a unique antisymmetric invariant bilinear form; in dimension two its tensor line is exactly the singlet line. Thus the actual binary instrument on representation registers is the nondestructive singlet/triplet instrument. I read Rudolph–Virmani v3, including its resource statement, preparation argument, and polynomial success/accuracy estimates. It needs only maximally mixed initial single qubits and adaptive singlet/triplet tests on arbitrary pairs, as claimed here.

Independent singlet cups with one member inactive give the required product maximally mixed reduced state. For each history the unnormalized reduced state is K_h rho K_h^*, and the same formula follows by tracing the untouched partners in the purified protocol. Its trace also gives the same outcome probability. Leaving discarded systems untouched implements the same subsequent reduced channels. All projectors commute with the diagonal G action; all required routing consists of swaps with polynomial overhead. This proves the reduction without postselection or hidden ancillary resources. Conversely, singlet preparation and the binary two-qubit projector have constant-size circuit implementations (a Bell-basis change and coherent binary flag measurement suffice), giving the stated BQP upper bound for the specified pair-only model. I also checked the Q8 matrices, their multiplication, and the group-average projector. It is exactly (II-XX-YY-ZZ)/4=P_s. Squaring the representation character gives the sum of the four one-dimensional characters; their projectors are the four Bell lines. The resolved and binary conclusions are therefore compatible.
